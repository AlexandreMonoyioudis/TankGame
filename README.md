A mobile top-down stage defense strategy game developed in C#. Players defend a central point against progressively challenging waves of enemy forces using diverse tank units and tactical air support.

---

## 🎮 Overview & Gameplay

In **Tank Game**, you are tasked with defending a core location from incoming enemy waves. The game challenges players to make quick tactical decisions under pressure:

* **Central Defense:** Protect your central base at all costs from invading ground forces.
* **Tank Diversity:** Deploy and upgrade a wide variety of tank types, each suited for different combat scenarios.
* **Air Support:** Call in strategic air strikes to turn the tide when enemy waves become overwhelming.
* **Strategic Progression:** Adapt your defense strategy as enemy forces increase in strength and number.
